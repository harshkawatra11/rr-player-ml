# Product

## Register
product

## What it is
A decision dashboard for the SupeRR Selector Season 2 hackathon (Rajasthan Royals and Unstop, Round 1). It shows how batters and bowlers perform under pressure in IPL and T20I data, ranks the most reliable players, and lets an analyst reproduce every number.

## Users
Rajasthan Royals judges and cricket analysts, on a desktop or laptop, usually with the methodology report open beside it. They are trained to distrust a single headline number, so every figure needs its unit, its sample size and its uncertainty within one glance.

## Purpose
Make the evidence for each rank inspectable: what counts as pressure, how a ball is valued, how sure the model is, and what it means for the Royals at the IPL 2027 auction.

## Tone and voice
A strategy and analytics team writing to a franchise's cricket operations group. First person plural, plain words, a number in most sentences. No hooks, no exclamation marks, no dashes.

## Visual direction
Consulting-grade and flat: square corners, thin rules, tables and charts carrying the argument, action-title headings with a source line. Rajasthan Royals pink #E50695 and navy #021D45 with blue #1226AA. No curves: no rounded pills, no smoothed chart lines, no gradients, no glass.

## Anti-references
Generic SaaS dashboards with rows of identical metric cards, rounded pill badges, gradient accents, smoothed area charts, and decorative motion.

## Strategic principles
- Every claim is a sentence with a number, a unit and a source.
- Uncertainty is shown next to every rank, never hidden.
- Tables carry rank and evidence; charts carry shape.
- One accent colour per meaning: pink is pressure, blue is ordinary, navy is structure.
